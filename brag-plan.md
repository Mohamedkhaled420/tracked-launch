# brag-plan.md — tracked.

> Generated by following the `/brag-slim` creative playbook (no Hyperframes; built entirely on-machine with FFmpeg + Playwright + TTS).

## 1. What is it

**tracked.** — a tracker app. It tracks things. You tell it what to track, and it tracks it.

> *User brief*: "My idea is a tracker app." Invention territory: I am designing the product, the UI, the copy, and the share copy from scratch. The yc-parody tone is honest about this — the joke is that the launch language is so confident it almost doesn't matter what the product does.

## 2. Who it's for / what it does for them

For people who would, in 2016, have downloaded a productivity app with a one-word name and a missing vowel. It tracks things so they don't have to think about the things they're tracking.

## 3. What sets it apart

Nothing, honestly. That's the bit.

## 4. Most impressive / funniest claim

"It tracks things. So you don't have to." — followed by "The line goes up." The parody is that none of these are real claims.

## 5. Visual hook

Pure black screen. White lowercase text fades up center: **we built a thing.** Two seconds of nothing. Hard cut.

## 6. Real UI / flow to show

Invented UI (since the source is an idea, not a codebase):
- A simple list view titled "things i'm tracking" with three items, each with a green checkmark and a timestamp
- A "Saved." toast that appears after a simulated click
- A single line chart going up-and-to-the-right for no stated reason

Per brag-slim's Show-the-thing law: when the source is an idea in text, inventing the UI is the explicit fallback. No invented metrics, no invented testimonials, no fake "10x faster" numbers — just the deadpan visual of a thing working.

## 7. Tone

`yc-parody` — deadpan 2016-era Series A startup energy, played completely straight.
- 5 scenes, one claim each
- Hard cuts between scenes
- Lots of negative space
- Lowercase type, period after the brand name
- No exclamation marks anywhere. Ever.

## 8. One-line share caption

> tracked. it tracks things. so you don't have to. now in private beta. backed by people you've heard of.

## 9. Visual identity

- **Background**: pure black `#0A0A0A` for hook/reveal/punchline scenes; pure white `#FFFFFF` for UI demo scenes (the contrast itself is the joke)
- **Type**: Inter (loaded from Google Fonts via Playwright); weight 600 for headlines, weight 400 for body, mono (JetBrains Mono) for the "timestamp" details in the UI demo
- **Brand mark**: lowercase `tracked.` — the period is part of the logo
- **Accent**: yc green `#00C805` (the chart-line / checkmark color — the only saturated thing on screen)
- **Motion**: slow fades within a scene (400ms ease-out), hard cuts between scenes (0ms)
- **Pacing**: every text line settles and holds at least ~1.2s before the cut

## 12. Music + SFX mix

- **Music**: single sustained synth pad in A minor, very slow pulse (~60bpm), low-passed so it sits under the voice. The deadpan tone earns a near-empty track.
- **SFX** (all under the music, low gain):
  - 6 soft clicks, one at the start of each hard cut (0.6s, 2.5s, 5.5s, 9.0s, 12.0s, 15.0s)
  - 3 ticks during scene 3 (one per list-row appearance, 200ms apart starting at 5.8s)
  - 1 soft pop at the toast in scene 4 (around 10.0s)
  - 1 pencil-draw tick sustained during scene 5's chart draw (12.0s–14.5s)
- **Mix**: music at -18 dB, SFX at -24 dB, voiceover at -6 dB (ducked over music via sidechain).

## 14. Creative laws checklist

- [x] **Short** — 18s, inside the 15-25s envelope
- [x] **Clear to a stranger** — by the end you know it's a tracker app called `tracked.`, in private beta, backed by anonymous VCs
- [x] **The hook is everything** — first 2s is pure black + lowercase claim, forces attention
- [x] **Show the thing** — scenes 3 + 4 show the actual (invented, but plausible) UI doing its job
- [x] **Specific** — no "streamline your workflow"; everything is in `tracked.`'s own deadpan register
- [x] **Readable** — every text line settles and holds ≥1.2s; the only fast motion is the row stagger (still 200ms per row, fully visible)
- [x] **Make it alive** — list rows appear one by one, toast slides up, chart line draws itself
- [x] **Funny earns its place** — the humor comes from the absurd confidence, not from jokes
- [x] **Every frame postable** — every scene is a clean centered composition with the brand mark visible
